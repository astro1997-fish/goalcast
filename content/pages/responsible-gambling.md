GoalCast is for adults aged 18 and over. Football predictions are meant to make watching the game more interesting. They are not a way to earn a living, clear a debt or solve a money problem.

## What the numbers really mean

- A probability is not a promise. A pick rated 85% still loses about one time in seven.
- Accumulators lose far more often than their individual legs. Five 85% picks together land less than half the time.
- Bookmakers build a margin into every price. Over time, most people who bet lose money. Our own model does not beat the bookmakers, and the [model page](page:model) says so.

## Keeping control

- **Set a budget before you start** and treat it as the cost of entertainment. When it is gone, stop.
- **Never chase losses.** Betting more to win back what you lost is how small losses become large ones.
- **Do not bet with money you need** for rent, food, bills or debts, and never with borrowed money.
- **Set a time limit** as well as a money limit.
- **Do not bet when upset, stressed or after drinking.**
- **Take breaks.** Most licensed bookmakers let you set deposit limits, time-outs and self-exclusion. Use them.

## Signs that betting is becoming a problem

- spending more money or time on betting than you intended
- hiding your betting from family or friends, or lying about it
- borrowing, selling things or missing bills to fund it
- feeling anxious, irritable or low when you try to stop
- betting to escape worries or to recover earlier losses

If any of these sound familiar, talking to someone helps, and the sooner the better.

## Where to get help

These services are free and confidential.

- **Gambling Therapy** — online support worldwide, in many languages: [gamblingtherapy.org](https://www.gamblingtherapy.org)
- **Gamblers Anonymous** — meetings in many countries: [gamblersanonymous.org](https://www.gamblersanonymous.org)
- **BeGambleAware** (United Kingdom): [begambleaware.org](https://www.begambleaware.org)
- **GamCare** (United Kingdom): [gamcare.org.uk](https://www.gamcare.org.uk)
- **National Council on Problem Gambling** (United States): call or text 1-800-GAMBLER, or visit [ncpgambling.org](https://www.ncpgambling.org)

If you are in immediate distress or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency services now.

## Under 18

It is illegal for anyone under 18 to gamble in most countries. If you share a device with children, consider parental-control software that blocks gambling sites.
